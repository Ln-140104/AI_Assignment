# AI Usage Disclosure

## Purpose

AI tools were used as a research and development assistant during
the investigation.

AI assistance was not treated as evidence.

## Uses of AI

AI assistance may be used for:

- brainstorming experimental hypotheses;
- identifying potential implementation issues;
- suggesting alternative metrics;
- explaining technical concepts;
- generating initial code scaffolding;
- reviewing calculations;
- improving documentation structure.

## Verification Policy

Any numerical result, experimental observation, or technical
conclusion included in the final submission must be independently
verified using the project code, supplied evidence, or an
authoritative source.

AI-generated claims are not considered evidence.
